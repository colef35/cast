Cast a net where the fish already are

Ads shout at everyone and hope. CAST listens for the people already raising their hand — then helps you answer like a human, at machine speed.

1 · LISTEN

Always on watch

CAST monitors the communities where your buyers decide — starting with Reddit — for your keywords, your competitors, and the problems your product solves.

2 · SCORE

Intent, ranked

Every conversation is scored by buying intent. "What do you guys use for X?" outranks a hundred casual mentions. Noise gets dropped, not delivered.

3 · DRAFT

Your voice, written for you

CAST drafts a genuinely helpful reply in your voice — helpful first, product second. Two or three variants, ready when you are.

4 · APPROVE

You're the boss

Every reply lands in your queue. Approve it, edit it, or kill it. Nothing ever posts behind your back — until you set the rules that let it.

5 · ENGAGE

Posted right

Replies go out inside each community's rules and rate limits, with disclosure controls you set. Built to earn trust, not bans.

6 · LEARN

Smarter every week

CAST tracks every reply's life — upvotes, responses, removals, sales — and feeds it back into the scoring. Your net learns where your fish bite.
