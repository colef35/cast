Straight answers

Is this just a spam bot with better manners?

No. Spam blasts the same pitch at everyone. CAST listens for people who are actively asking, writes a reply that answers their actual question, and shows it to you before anything posts. If a community doesn't welcome it, CAST doesn't send it.

Will this get my Reddit account banned?

That risk is the whole reason the leash exists. Rule checks before drafting, human approval before posting, human pacing, and a ban-risk meter that throttles CAST before any platform does. Accounts get banned for spraying; CAST doesn't spray.

Do I have to write anything?

No. You give CAST your product and a few samples of how you talk. It drafts, you tap approve. Most owners spend a few minutes a day in the queue — usually with coffee, usually smiling at what it caught overnight.

What platforms does it watch?

Reddit first, because that's where the buying questions live. Hacker News, Bluesky, and YouTube comments are next; X and LinkedIn land on the Autopilot tier.
